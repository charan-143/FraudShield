Decision brief

Extra trees was selected using the tune period. On the final held-out test it achieved AP 0.7898, with an exploratory fixed-block resampling interval 0.6668–0.8694. At a top-1% review budget its recall was 83.8%. This is a retrospective research benchmark, not approval for deployment.

Protocol

split: Ordered time quantiles: train 50%, tune 15%, calibration 15%, test 20%; tied timestamps kept together.

calibration: Sigmoid fit on independent calibration period. Threshold from same period targets 1% alerts; actual test alert rate can differ.

uncertainty: 150 fixed non-overlapping-block resamples (40 contiguous blocks), percentile 95% AP interval; conditional on this fitted model.

latency: 12 local repetitions of a 256-row batch; includes feature validation and calibration; excludes network and disk.

average_precision: scikit-learn average_precision_score; step-weighted AP, not trapezoidal PR area.

Limits and decisions

  • Two days of European transactions from 2013 do not establish current bank or Indian payment performance.
  • Original PCA fitting provenance is outside this experiment; entity identifiers and label delay are unavailable.
  • PCA components cannot be translated into merchant, device, customer or behavioral explanations.
  • Fraud labels are historical outcomes, not proof of criminal intent; scores require human review.
  • Exposure is the sum of historical fraud transaction amounts; it is not realized savings or recoverable loss.
  • Local results are not directly comparable to published random-split or Kaggle hidden-test scores.
  • Block bootstrap intervals describe this short test period only; few frauds and dependent events limit precision.
  • Protected attributes are unavailable; demographic fairness cannot be measured on this dataset.

Uncertainty guards

BAF has one test-month cluster per fold; ULB's final test has 1 elapsed-day cluster(s). Both fail the default minimum of 20 dependence units. No dependence-supported AP interval is produced. Existing ULB fixed-block resampling intervals remain exploratory historical evidence only.

Automated tests cover data and label chronology, review identity/integrity, amount precision and currency separation, protocol gates, model selection, SHAP additivity, HTTP handlers and dashboard logic. Live browser/network and current-bank deployment validation are not claimed.

Balancing fraud capture and customer interruption

Calibration-only frontier. Illustrative research objective: at least 80% recall, with at most 50 legitimate interruptions per 10,000 legitimate transactions. The current model cannot satisfy both on calibration data. No new policy is promoted. Only 49 calibration fraud cases support these estimates.
