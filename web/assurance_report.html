Uncertainty guards

BAF has one test-month cluster per fold; ULB's final test has 1 elapsed-day cluster(s). Both fail the default minimum of 20 dependence units. No dependence-supported AP interval is produced. Existing ULB fixed-block resampling intervals remain exploratory historical evidence only.
