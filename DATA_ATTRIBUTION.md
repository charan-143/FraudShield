# Data and derived-artifact attribution

## Feedzai BAF

Bank Account Fraud Dataset Suite (NeurIPS 2022), Base variant, Kaggle release 2. Original source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sgpjesus/bank-account-fraud-dataset-neurips-2022 . Metadata reports last update 2023-11-29, not a 2026 observation period. Downloaded original Base.csv SHA-256: `7bf10a37ce07e72e14c1b09e5efee3d27261baff4facc7da767b0474dcf9b809`.

Creators: Sergio Jesus, Jose Pombal, Duarte Alves, Andre Cruz, Pedro Saleiro, Rita P. Ribeiro, Joao Gama, Pedro Bizarro. Paper: *Turning the Tables: Biased, Imbalanced, Dynamic Tabular Datasets for ML Evaluation*, NeurIPS 2022. Owner documentation: https://github.com/feedzai/bank-account-fraud . The source suite is generated from anonymized real account-opening data; the released records are synthetic.

The dataset's Kaggle metadata states **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/ . BAF-derived study outputs, explanation examples and the bundled BAF trained model in this delivery are shared under those noncommercial/share-alike conditions. Changes here include exclusion of age/month/device_fraud_count from predictors, fixed forward train/tune/calibration/test splits, local models, probability calibration, group audits, and model contributions. No creator endorsement is implied. Raw data is not redistributed in the archive. Commercial deployment requires data and permissions suitable for that use; these artifacts do not supply them.
